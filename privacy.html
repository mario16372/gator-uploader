Privacy Policy — Gator Uploader

Effective date: October 28, 2026

Summary

Gator Uploader is a personal tool its owner uses to upload their own videos to their own TikTok account as drafts. It collects the minimum information TikTok requires to do that, keeps it on the owner's own computer, and never sells or shares it.

What information is accessed

When the owner connects their TikTok account through TikTok's Login Kit, Gator Uploader receives:

  • the TikTok account's basic identifier (open ID) and display name, to confirm which account is connected; and
  • an access token that allows uploading videos to that account's drafts (the `video.upload` permission).

Gator Uploader does not access followers, messages, comments, contacts, analytics, location or any other account data, and it does not access any other person's information.

How the information is used

Only to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's TikTok drafts.

Where it is stored

The access token is stored only on the owner's personal computer. It is not sent to any server other than TikTok's own API.

Sharing

The information is never sold, rented or shared with anyone.

Retention and deletion

The token is kept only while the tool is in use. Disconnecting the app in TikTok (Settings > Security and permissions > Apps and services) revokes it, and the owner deletes the stored token when they stop using the tool.

Children

Gator Uploader is not directed at children and is used only by its adult owner.

Changes

This policy may be updated. The effective date above shows when it last changed.
